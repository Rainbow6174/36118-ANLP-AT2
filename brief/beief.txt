AT2 Part A: Group project poster

Type:                   Group, poster

Length:               PPT slide or digital poster

Weight:              10%

Student teams should present an overview of their project work including project goals, techniques used, outcomes, and achievements in a digital poster. Submission includes the digital version of your poster (Source PPT/ Canva/ other files) on canvas, there is no need to print the poster. While typically used in research conferences, posters are an effective way to convey complex work in simple, easy-to-read formats - see examples hereLinks to an external site.https://guides.nyu.edu/posters. We recommend using Landscape orientation for your poster since it will be displayed via a pod in the final showcase day (although portrait orientation is fine as well).

Note: The teaching team will nominate exceptional posters for the Final showcase based on the quality of work. Two teams are awarded ‘Best Project Awards’ in the Final Subject Showcase by an external judge and provided certificates.

AT2 Part B: Group project report and code (Individual submission)
Due 14 Oct by 23:59 Points 100 Submitting a file upload Available after 10 Aug at 0:00
Weight: 40%

Group & Individual Assignment

Project report (everyone in the group must submit), plus peer evaluation (completed on SparkPlus)

Task
As part of this task, student teams should develop a web-based application for a specific problem/ user group, using NLP based methods. Submission includes a detailed technical report on their project work highlighting all their steps and findings from the start to the end. Teams can choose one of the problem statements provided by the teaching team or choose to develop their own application. Group members should make sure that everyone contributes to the report equally (which is also self-assessed by all team members). The technical report should include the elements below, as appropriate:

Project objectives and scope
Project phases and contributions by individual team members
Data sources
NLP methods and techniques used, including justification for choices made
Findings from the project
Outcomes and value added
Challenges faced and solutions derived
Recommendations and next steps
Appendix (for code, visualizations, and other material requiring an extended number of pages)
Team members will also evaluate each other based on their contributions to the project. This survey will run on SPARKPlus, a tool for self and peer assessment and feedback. Peer rating provides weightage for the overall project evaluation and should be completed by all team members (criteria below):
